About me

I’m a PhD student in Data Science at the University of Rome Tor Vergata. I study how language models use evidence. When a model reads a claim alongside a passage, can it tell whether the passage supports the claim, contradicts it, or leaves the question open?

My background is in bioinformatics and clinical biostatistics. At Bambino Gesù Children’s Hospital, I worked with clinical researchers to analyse immune responses and protein profiles in paediatric cohorts.

What I’m working on

I use FrameNet to study how language models interpret events and the roles of their participants. I also evaluate how reliably models detect inconsistencies in contracts.

In bioinformatics, I’ve built a prototype for finding related proteins in Swiss-Prot using both amino acid sequences and the functional descriptions written by curators.
